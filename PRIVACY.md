# LocalLab privacy policy

**Effective 18 September 2026** · applies to LocalLab (listed on the Mac App Store as
"LocalLab AI") version 1.0 and later.

LocalLab runs AI models on your Mac. It is designed so that your data stays there.

## What the developer collects

**Nothing.** LocalLab has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The
developer runs no server that the app contacts, and receives no information about you or how
you use the app.

## What stays on your Mac

Everything you create or provide in LocalLab is stored only on your Mac:

- **Conversations** with chat models, and **images you attach** to them.
- **Images and videos you upscale**, and the results.
- **Models** you download, in the library folder you choose.
- **Measurements of your Mac** — a short set of speed tests run on first launch, and the speed
  and memory use of the jobs you run — used only to choose settings for your Mac.
- **Your settings.**

None of this is uploaded anywhere. You can delete models and outputs in Settings → Storage,
delete conversations in Chat, and remove everything else by deleting the app.

## What leaves your Mac

LocalLab connects to one service: **Hugging Face** (huggingface.co), where AI models are
published. It does so only to:

- **search** for models when you use Browse, and
- **download** the files of a model you choose to install.

These requests go directly from your Mac to Hugging Face. Like any website, Hugging Face
receives your IP address and the requests themselves — the search you typed, or the name of the
model being downloaded. Their handling of that information is governed by
[Hugging Face's privacy policy](https://huggingface.co/privacy). The contents of your
conversations, images, videos and results are never sent.

LocalLab doesn't ask for a Hugging Face account. Should a future version let you add a Hugging
Face access token (for models that require one), it will be kept in your Mac's Keychain and sent
only to Hugging Face, with requests for those models.

## Models and their licences

Models are made by third parties and published on Hugging Face under their own licences, which
LocalLab shows before you install a model. Models run entirely on your Mac; the developers of a
model receive nothing from your use of it.

## Children

LocalLab collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If LocalLab ever changes what it sends or stores — for example, an optional way to share
anonymous performance measurements to improve its estimates for other Macs — it will be off
unless you turn it on, it will be explained in the app before it does anything, and this
policy will be updated first. The effective date above shows the latest version.
